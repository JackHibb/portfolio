About

I am a Business Analytics major at Miami University with a minor in Information Systems, graduating in May 2027. Most of my coursework sits at the point where data meets decisions: databases and SQL, regression, business intelligence and visualization, and enterprise systems. I like the part of analytics where a messy set of records turns into something a manager can actually act on, whether that is a dashboard, a query, or a simple summary of what changed and why. Outside of class I have spent several summers in service and maintenance jobs where I had to triage requests, work with a small team, and talk to people who did not care about the process, only the result. That has shaped how I approach analysis: start with the question the person actually has, then work backward to the data. I am a member of the Sports Analytics and Data Club, and I also built a responsive website for a local landscaping business. I am looking for an internship where I can keep building on SQL, Python, and business intelligence tools while learning how analytics is used day to day.
