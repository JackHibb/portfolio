About

I am a Business Analytics major at Miami University with minors in Information Systems and Sport Analytics, graduating in May 2027. This semester I am taking the applied core of the major at once: applied regression, business intelligence and data visualization, enterprise systems, monitoring and design, and the ISA capstone. Most weeks that means taking a messy set of records and turning it into a query, a model, or a chart that answers one specific question a person actually asked.

Outside of class I build for real businesses. I am building the e-commerce site for Flow State Vintage, a vintage shop in Oxford, with Stripe checkout so the shop can keep selling over winter and summer break when the students leave town and can reach buyers for one-of-one pieces that will not move locally. Before that I designed a responsive site for Buckeye Home & Lawn Care in Fairfield.

The rest of my work history is service and maintenance: 15 to 20 requests a week across a 30-acre campus, 100 or more guests a day at a golf course. Those jobs are where I learned to triage a queue, work with a small team, and talk to people who do not care about the process, only the result. That is how I approach analysis too. Start with the question the person actually has, then work backward to the data. I am looking for an analytics internship or analyst role where I can keep building on SQL, Python, and BI tools, with a longer-term interest in sports analytics.
